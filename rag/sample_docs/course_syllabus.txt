CS771 - Introduction to Machine Learning
Course Syllabus and Exam Information
Professor: Dr. Ayesha Sharma, IIT Delhi
Semester: August-December 2026

Course Overview:
This course provides a comprehensive introduction to machine learning concepts, algorithms, and applications. Students will learn both the theoretical foundations and practical implementation of ML techniques.

Prerequisites:
- Linear Algebra (matrices, vectors, eigenvalues)
- Probability and Statistics (Bayes' theorem, distributions)
- Python programming (NumPy, Pandas, Matplotlib)

Grading Policy:
- Assignments (4 total): 40%

- Final Exam: 30%
- Project: 10%

Important Dates:
- Mid-term Exam: October 15, 2026

Final Exam Details:
The final exam covers chapters 5 through 10 of the course material, with a focus on machine learning algorithms and their computational complexity. The exam is open book, 3 hours duration. You may bring printed notes and textbooks, but no electronic devices are allowed. The exam will include both theoretical questions (proofs, derivations) and practical questions (algorithm design, code analysis).

Topics covered in the final exam:
1. Support Vector Machines and Kernel Methods
2. Decision Trees and Random Forests
3. Neural Networks and Backpropagation
4. Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs)
5. Recurrent Neural Networks (RNNs) and LSTMs
6. Regularization techniques (L1, L2, Dropout)
